# Standard Auto Policy

### A1 Collision
We cover direct physical damage to the insured vehicle caused by collision with another vehicle or object. The policy deductible applies.

### A2 Comprehensive
We cover damage to the insured vehicle from causes other than collision, including falling objects, windstorm, hail, fire, vandalism and theft. The policy deductible applies.

### A3 Theft
Theft of the entire vehicle is covered under Comprehensive. A police report must be filed and its number provided to us. Personal belongings left inside the vehicle are not covered under this policy.

### A4 Accident Benefits
We pay reasonable medical and rehabilitation expenses for the insured driver injured in an auto accident, regardless of fault, subject to medical documentation.

### A5 Glass
Windshield and window damage is covered under Comprehensive. The deductible is waived for repairs but applies to full replacement.

### A6 Not-at-Fault Collision
If an identified third-party driver is entirely at fault for a collision, the insured's deductible is waived.

### A7 Exclusions
We do not cover: racing or speed contests; use of the vehicle for paid delivery or ridesharing without an endorsement; intentional damage; operation by an unlicensed driver; mechanical breakdown or wear and tear.

### A8 Claim Conditions
Losses must be reported within 7 days. The insured must provide photos, repair estimates and any police report numbers on request.
