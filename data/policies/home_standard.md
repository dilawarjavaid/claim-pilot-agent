# Standard Home Policy

### H1 Dwelling
We cover sudden and accidental direct physical loss to the dwelling, subject to the exclusions in this policy. The policy deductible applies.

### H2 Water Damage
We cover sudden and accidental escape of water from plumbing, heating or appliances, including resulting damage to floors and cabinets. We do not cover gradual leaks, seepage, or damage from lack of maintenance. Overland flood and sewer backup are excluded unless an endorsement is purchased.

### H3 Windstorm and Hail
We cover damage to the dwelling caused by windstorm or hail, including roofs, siding, windows and skylights.

### H4 Theft of Personal Property
We cover theft of personal property following forcible entry. Special limit: jewellery, watches and furs are limited to $5,000 in total per claim unless individually scheduled. A police report is required.

### H5 Contents
Personal property is covered up to the contents limit shown on the declarations page, on a replacement cost basis once items are replaced.

### H6 Exclusions
We do not cover: wear and tear; mould or rot; intentional loss; earthquake; loss occurring while the home has been vacant for more than 30 consecutive days.

### H7 Claim Conditions
The insured must report losses promptly, take reasonable steps to prevent further damage (for example shutting off water), and provide receipts or proof of ownership on request.
